# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML, CSS and JavaScript so the first version can be opened directly in a browser without a build step.

## Users

Potential clients of an independent web developer: companies, professionals and small businesses that need a credible digital presence, a product catalog or an online sales structure.

## Product Purpose

This is a commercial presentation page for JV / João Victor, designed to help a prospective client quickly understand the available web solutions, scope, prices, process, timing and how to start a project.

## Positioning

A small digital studio experience with direct, understandable offers for businesses at different stages of digital presence, without pretending to be a large agency or fabricating client proof.

## Operating Context

The page is shared through WhatsApp, Instagram or in person. Visitors should be able to scan it on mobile, compare plans and use a CTA to start a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The page must be responsive, accessible and usable without a backend. WhatsApp, Instagram and email are intentionally left as editable placeholders. Pricing is current as provided in the brief. No real portfolio projects, clients, phone numbers or payment integrations may be invented.

## Brand Commitments

The brand is JV, with João Victor / Desenvolvimento Web as the descriptor. The visual direction is sophisticated, minimal, premium, editorial and spacious, using an off-white, near-black, muted gold and light gray palette.

## Evidence on Hand

No real projects, logo file, social handles, phone number or email were supplied. Portfolio entries must remain concept projects or “Projeto em breve”.

## Product Principles

- Explain the offer quickly and honestly.
- Make scope and price easy to compare.
- Show craft through the interface itself.
- Keep the path from interest to contact direct.
- Avoid absolute promises and invented proof.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The page must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion preferences and mobile layouts.
